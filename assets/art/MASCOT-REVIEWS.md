# nisse mascot: the independent critique reviews (N-402)

Two independent art-director reviews, one per round, each by a fresh reviewer who saw only the sheets and the logo.
Compiled here so round 3 and the final sheet keep the findings. ASCII only.

## Round 1 (four options: classic, wee, wanderer, faceted)

Ranking: wanderer, wee, classic, faceted. No keeper; round 2 needed.
- Wanderer's hill-peek badge is the one ownable idea (it echoes the logo disc and hill).
- Wee has the only face that reads at 48 px (eyes, a nose in a different tone from the skin, cheeks).
- Classic is the standard garden gnome; the dark wedges beside the nose read as sunglasses; the beard hides the torso.
- Faceted reads as a wizard; the cone hat is not a tomte cap; no face.
- Pickaxe read as an umbrella: the head must be much bigger, in steel, two-sided. Hands were bare circles.
- Blue and green robes fight the brand. Pompom reads Santa. Lantern and backpack unreadable: cut.
- The four were really two gnomes recoloured.

## Round 2 (eight options: peeker, tomte, miner, bean, block, sleepy, cheeky, tinker)

Ranking: tomte, tinker, sleepy, bean, cheeky, peeker, miner, block.
- Tomte is the master: hooked cap tip matches the logo, eyes under the brim, nose out of a long beard, reads at 24 px.
  Weak points: almost no expression, beard too tall (a white slab).
- Tinker: goggles and a moustache are the most ownable accessory; blue lenses survive as dots at 24 px.
- Sleepy: the most charm (closed smiling eyes) but the face vanishes at 24 px and the bent cap is not the brand atom.
- Dead ends: block (stiff), miner (elf ears, broken sleeve, overalls leave the robe silhouette), and the peeker figure.
- Blue (cheeky) and mustard (bean) robes clash; grey works; dark robes vanish on Ink (give them an edge or a Paper hem).
- The pickaxe was still part umbrella: angle the head, one pointed side and one flat adze, thicker warm handle, a hand
  clearly wrapped round it, a visible sleeve and arm.
- Avatar badge: tighter crop, cap bleeding off the top, face and beard filling the disc; the hill-and-stars disc ties it to the logo.
- The set was not different enough: three of the eight were one gnome, two shared the elf-ear head.

## What round 3 does with it

Four variants of the round-2 tomte (tomte, wee tomte, digger, elder) plus a waving pose: beard to chest length, brows or a
smile, no elf ears, grey and white robes with edges on the dark one, a rebuilt pickaxe, and the hill-and-stars badge crop.
